01 / The thesis of the letter

ROMANS 1:16–17 · KJV
SCRIPTURE ANCHOR
“For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ: for it is the power of God unto salvation to every one that believeth;”
ROMANS 1:16 · KJV (EXCERPT)

Paul’s opening thesis frames the whole vision: the gospel reveals the righteousness of God “from faith to faith” (1:17). The chapters move from the universal need for salvation to the greatness of God’s mercy.

02 / The eleven-chapter scripture map

CHAPTER-BY-CHAPTER · KJV EXCERPTS
ROMANS 01 / 1:16–17

THE GOSPEL REVEALED

The power of God unto salvation

Paul declares the gospel of Christ, righteousness revealed by faith, and humanity’s accountability before God.

“The just shall live by faith.”
ROMANS 1:1:17 · KJV
ROMANS 02 / 2:1–16, 25–29

THE RIGHTEOUS JUDGE

No partiality with God

God judges according to truth, not outward status; true inward obedience matters more than outward religious identity.

“For there is no respect of persons with God.”
ROMANS 2:2:11 · KJV
ROMANS 03 / 3:9–28

ALL UNDER SIN

Justified freely by grace

Jew and Gentile alike are under sin. God’s righteousness is manifested through faith in Jesus Christ; boasting is excluded.

“For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God;”
ROMANS 3:3:23 · KJV
ROMANS 04 / 4:1–25

ABRAHAM BELIEVED

Faith counted for righteousness

Abraham is presented as an example of righteousness reckoned by faith before circumcision, and of hope in God’s promise.

“Abraham believed God, and it was counted unto him for righteousness.”
ROMANS 4:4:3 · KJV
ROMANS 05 / 5:1–21

PEACE THROUGH CHRIST

From Adam to Christ

Being justified by faith, believers have peace with God through Jesus Christ; Paul contrasts Adam’s offence with Christ’s gift.

“Therefore being justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ:”
ROMANS 5:5:1 · KJV
ROMANS 06 / 6:1–23

DEAD TO SIN

Alive unto God

Union with Christ in His death and resurrection calls believers to refuse sin’s reign and yield themselves to righteousness.

“For sin shall not have dominion over you: for ye are not under the law, but under grace.”
ROMANS 6:6:14 · KJV
ROMANS 07 / 7:1–25

THE INNER STRUGGLE

The law and the human condition

Paul describes the law’s goodness, sin’s misuse of the commandment, and the struggle of desiring good yet encountering sin.

“O wretched man that I am! who shall deliver me from the body of this death?”
ROMANS 7:7:24 · KJV
ROMANS 08 / 8:1–39

LIFE IN THE SPIRIT

No condemnation in Christ

The Spirit brings life and adoption; creation groans, the Spirit helps in weakness, and nothing separates believers from God’s love in Christ.

“There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus,”
ROMANS 8:8:1 (excerpt) · KJV
ROMANS 09 / 9:1–33

GOD’S SOVEREIGN PURPOSE

Mercy and promise

Paul grieves for Israel and considers God’s purpose in election, mercy, and the distinction between natural descent and promise.

“I will have mercy on whom I will have mercy,”
ROMANS 9:9:15 (excerpt) · KJV
ROMANS 10 / 10:1–21

THE WORD OF FAITH

Christ, the end of the law

Paul expresses his desire for Israel’s salvation and emphasizes righteousness by faith, confession, belief, and the hearing of God’s word.

“So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.”
ROMANS 10:10:17 · KJV
ROMANS 11 / 11:1–36

MERCY & THE MYSTERY

The olive tree and God’s wisdom

Paul describes a remnant by grace, warns Gentiles against boasting, speaks of Israel’s future mercy, and ends in worship.

“For of him, and through him, and to him, are all things:”
ROMANS 11:11:36 (excerpt) · KJV

03 / Four movements of the gospel

THE ARGUMENT · NOT A REPLACEMENT FOR READING THE TEXT
MOVEMENT 01
The need for righteousnessRomans 1–3 · Sin and judgment; righteousness of God through faith; no boasting.
MOVEMENT 02
The gift received by faithRomans 4–5 · Abraham believed; justification, peace, hope, and grace through Christ.
MOVEMENT 03
A transformed lifeRomans 6–8 · Dead to sin, conflict with sin, and life and assurance through the Spirit.
MOVEMENT 04
God’s purpose in mercyRomans 9–11 · Israel, Gentiles, promise, the olive tree, and the wisdom of God.

04 / The central contrasts

THEOLOGICAL CONNECTIONS · SCRIPTURE REFERENCES
ROMANS 3–5

Works / Faith

NOT BY BOASTING

All have sinned; boasting is excluded (3:23, 27). Abraham was counted righteous through faith (4:3).

BY GRACE THROUGH CHRIST

Justified freely by grace through redemption in Christ (3:24); peace with God through Him (5:1).

ROMANS 5–8

Death / Life

IN ADAM / UNDER SIN

Sin entered by one man, and death by sin (5:12). Sin’s wages are death (6:23).

IN CHRIST / BY THE SPIRIT

Walk in newness of life (6:4); the Spirit of life in Christ makes free (8:2).

ROMANS 7–8

Struggle / Assurance

THE CONFLICT

Paul describes willing to do good while encountering the law of sin (7:18–25).

THE HOPE

No condemnation in Christ (8:1); the Spirit bears witness of adoption (8:15–16).

ROMANS 9–11

Warning / Mercy

DO NOT BOAST

Gentile branches are warned not to boast against the branches (11:17–22).

BEHOLD GOD’S WISDOM

Paul speaks of mercy toward Jew and Gentile and worships the depth of God’s wisdom (11:30–36).

06 / Reflect & respond

STUDY QUESTIONS · APPLICATION, NOT DIRECT QUOTATION
FAITH · 1–5
Where am I trusting in myself?

Consider the difference between boasting in works and receiving righteousness by faith.

WALK · 6–8
What does newness of life require?

Reflect on yielding to righteousness and walking after the Spirit.

HUMILITY · 9–11
How do I respond to mercy?

Consider Paul’s warning against boasting and his closing worship of God’s wisdom.
