CHAPTER 01 / THE FATHER'S PURPOSE

Blessed in Christ

Paul praises God for spiritual blessings in Christ (1:3): chosen in Him before the foundation of the world (1:4), predestinated unto adoption by Jesus Christ (1:5), and redeemed through His blood with forgiveness of sins (1:7).

CHAPTER 01 / ASSURANCE & AUTHORITY

Sealed with the Spirit

After hearing the word of truth and believing, the saints were sealed with the Holy Spirit of promise (1:13–14). Paul prays for wisdom and revelation, enlightened understanding, and knowledge of the power God worked in raising Christ (1:17–23).

“And hath put all things under his feet, and gave him to be the head over all things to the church,”
EPHESIANS 1:22 · KJV

03 / The mystery made known

EPHESIANS 3:1–13
CHAPTER 03 / REVELATION

Fellowheirs in one body

The mystery revealed by the Spirit is that the Gentiles should be fellowheirs, of the same body, and partakers of God's promise in Christ by the gospel (3:3–6). Paul describes his ministry of making this fellowship known (3:7–9).

“That the Gentiles should be fellowheirs, and of the same body...”
EPHESIANS 3:6 · KJV (EXCERPT)
CHAPTER 03 / PURPOSE

Wisdom displayed through the church

God's manifold wisdom is made known through the church to principalities and powers in heavenly places, according to His eternal purpose in Christ (3:10–11). In Christ we have boldness and access with confidence by faith (3:12).

FORMER DISTANCE

Strangers from the covenants of promise, without hope and without God in the world (2:12).

NEW ACCESS

Made nigh by the blood of Christ (2:13); access by one Spirit unto the Father (2:18).

05 / Live the vision

APPLICATION PROMPTS · NOT DIRECT QUOTATION
REMEMBER
What is my identity in Christ?

Reflect on redemption, adoption, and the sealing of the Spirit (1:4–14).

WALK
What does grace produce in me?

Consider the good works prepared for those made alive in Christ (2:8–10).

LOVE
Am I living as part of one body?

Seek peace and unity while growing rooted and grounded in Christ's love (2:14–22; 3:17–19).
