THE CENTRAL CONNECTION

Romans explains the need for righteousness and its gift through faith. Ephesians shows the resulting new identity and unity of God's people. Colossians focuses attention on Christ Himself, in whom the believer is complete. This is a thematic reading across the three letters, not a claim that they were written as a single continuous argument.

01 / The thesis of the letter

ROMANS 1:16–17 · KJV
SCRIPTURE ANCHOR
“For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ: for it is the power of God unto salvation to every one that believeth;”
ROMANS 1:16 · KJV (EXCERPT)

Paul’s opening thesis frames the whole vision: the gospel reveals the righteousness of God “from faith to faith” (1:17). The chapters move from the universal need for salvation to the greatness of God’s mercy.

ROMANS 4:3 · KJV
ROMANS 05 / 5:1–21

PEACE THROUGH CHRIST

ROMANS 06 / 6:1–23

ROMANS 6:14 · KJV

THE INNER STRUGGLE

Christ is before all things

Christ is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of every creature; all things were created by Him and for Him. He is before all things, and by Him all things consist (1:15–17).

From alienated to reconciled

Peace is made through the blood of His cross (1:20). Those once alienated and enemies in mind by wicked works are now reconciled through His death (1:21–22), with the call to continue grounded and settled in the faith (1:23).

03 / The battle for understanding

COLOSSIANS 2:4, 8, 16–23
THE WARNING

Do not be moved from Christ

  • Beware enticing words (2:4).
  • Beware philosophy and vain deceit according to human tradition, not Christ (2:8).
  • Let no man judge you in meat, drink, holyday, new moon, or sabbath days (2:16–17).
  • Do not let anyone beguile you through false humility and worshipping of angels (2:18).
  • Do not mistake man-made ordinances and self-imposed severity for true spiritual growth (2:20–23).
THE FOUNDATION

NOT THE FOUNDATION

Human tradition, persuasive words, ritual judgments, and outward displays of humility.

THE FOUNDATION

Christ, in whom are hid all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge (2:3); the Head from whom the body grows (2:19).

“For in him dwelleth all the fulness of the Godhead bodily.”
