The Thesis of Covenant Love

“the faithful God, which keepeth covenant and mercy with them that love him”DEUTERONOMY 7:9 (excerpt) · KJV

Covenant love is the Hebrew hesed, rendered mercy, kindness, lovingkindness and goodness. The Covenant Love study found it 248 times in 241 verses, and it stands in the same verse as “covenant” 13 times. It is love bound by promise, not by mood. In the New Testament the promise has a name: God sent His Son “to perform the mercy promised to our fathers, and to remember his holy covenant” (Luke 1:72). In Him the covenant love of the Father is made visible, sealed, and opened to all who abide in His love.

Four Movements of Covenant Love

01 / HESED

Steadfast Love

The LORD is “abundant in goodness and truth, keeping mercy for thousands” (Exodus 34:6–7). Hesed is love that has bound itself and does not let go.

02 / COVENANT

Bound by Promise

“My mercy will I keep for him for evermore, and my covenant shall stand fast with him” (Psalm 89:28). Covenant gives love its shape and its keeping.

03 / THE SON

Love Made Visible

“In this was manifested the love of God toward us, because that God sent his only begotten Son” (1 John 4:9). The covenant mercy has a face and a name.

04 / ABIDE

Kept in His Love

“As the Father hath loved me, so have I loved you: continue ye in my love” (John 15:9). Those received into the covenant are called to remain.

The Father · The Son · The Beloved

THE FATHER · HE KEEPS COVENANT

Faithful to a thousand generations

From the bow in the cloud to Sinai, from David's throne to the prophets, the Father binds Himself by promise and keeps mercy for thousands. Even when His people break the covenant, He answers with a new one (Jeremiah 31:31–33).

THE SON · HE SEALS IT

Mercy promised, mercy performed

The beloved Son, in whom the Father is well pleased, comes to perform the mercy promised to the fathers (Luke 1:72). He gives His life for His friends and gives the cup of the new testament in His blood (Luke 22:20).

THE BELOVED · WE ABIDE

Continue ye in my love

Those who receive Him are loved as the Father loves the Son (John 17:23). They keep His commandments, abide in His love, and love one another as He has loved them (John 15:9–12), persuaded that nothing can separate them from the love of God in Christ Jesus (Romans 8:39).

Live the Vision

Practical responses · applications, not quotations

  1. Remember His name

    Read Exodus 34:6–7 aloud this week and thank God for each part of the name He gave Himself: merciful, gracious, longsuffering, abundant in goodness and truth.

    EXODUS 34:6–7
  2. Receive new mercies daily

    Begin each morning by naming one mercy of God from the day before, and one thing you are trusting His faithfulness for today.

    LAMENTATIONS 3:22–23
  3. Look to the Son

    Read Luke 1:68–79 and John 3:16–17 side by side: the covenant promised, and the covenant given in the Son.

    LUKE 1:72 · JOHN 3:16
  4. Abide and keep

    Choose one commandment of Christ to keep deliberately this week, as a way of continuing in His love rather than earning it.

    JOHN 15:9–10
  5. Love as you are loved

    Show covenant kindness to one person who cannot repay it: faithful, patient, and kept even when it costs you.

    JOHN 15:12 · 1 JOHN 4:11

Reflection & Prayer

SELECT A REFLECTION

Where have you treated God's love as a feeling that comes and goes, rather than as hesed: love He has bound Himself to keep (Exodus 34:6–7)?

Prayer: LORD God, merciful and gracious, abundant in goodness and truth, thank You that You keep covenant and mercy, and that Your compassions fail not. Thank You for Your beloved Son, sent to perform the mercy promised to our fathers, whose blood is the blood of the everlasting covenant. Keep me in His love, and teach me to love as I have been loved. Amen.
